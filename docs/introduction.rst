# Introduction to OpenDose-PopPK

OpenDose-PopPK is an innovative framework for conducting population pharmacokinetic (PopPK) analyses. It provides researchers and clinicians with tools and resources to efficiently model drug behavior across diverse patient populations, facilitating a deeper understanding of pharmacokinetics.

## Features

- **User-Friendly Interface**: A robust GUI that simplifies the modeling process.
- **Flexible Modeling Options**: Supports a variety of statistical models suitable for pharmacokinetic analysis.
- **Integration with Existing Tools**: Can easily be combined with other pharmacometric software and tools.
- **Comprehensive Documentation**: Well-structured guides and tutorials to assist users at all levels.

## Purpose

The purpose of OpenDose-PopPK is to enhance the pharmacometric research landscape by providing an open-source solution that is both scientifically rigorous and accessible to a wide audience. Its design enables users to focus on their research rather than the underlying technical complexities.
